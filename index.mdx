MARS is a small mobile robot with an arm, cameras, and an onboard AI computer. It sees, talks, drives around your home, and manipulates objects. You program it by writing **agents** (a prompt plus a list of skills) and **skills** (Python code or trained policies). Everything that runs on it, [Innate OS](https://github.com/innate-inc/innate-os), is open source.
