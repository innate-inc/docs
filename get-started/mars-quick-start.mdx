This guide takes a new MARS from the box to running its first agent. You need the robot and a phone.

## 1. Power on and connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Power on">
    Put MARS on the floor and plug in the battery. The robot boots on its own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    <AppDownloadCards />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Open the app and follow onboarding. It finds MARS over Bluetooth and puts it on your Wi-Fi. Details: [Connect the phone app](/robots/apps#connect-the-phone-app).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 2. Update the robot

<Warning>
  Update before anything else. An outdated OS is the most common cause of problems on a new MARS.
</Warning>

Find the robot's IP in the app under **Configuration → WiFi**, then from your computer:

```bash
ssh jetson1@<robot-ip>     # password: goodbot<number>, e.g. goodbot21
innate update apply
```

More on updates: [Maintenance](/robots/mars/updates-and-maintenance).

## 3. Drive it

Open **Manual Control** in the app. Use the joystick to drive and the slider to tilt the head.

If you have the leader arm, plug it into your phone over USB-C and toggle **Arm**. MARS's arm mirrors yours. See [Driving and teleoperation](/robots/mars/control-and-connectivity).

MARS maps the room automatically the first time it connects. To start a new map later, go to **Configuration → Mapping → Create New Map** and drive around.

## 4. Talk to it

Put MARS in front of you, open **Agentic** in the app, and ask *"What do you see?"* Its thoughts and answers appear in the chat.

Then try navigation: *"Move forward one meter"*, *"Go to the fridge"*, or *"Explore until you see a person."*

## 5. Run a skill and an agent

- **Run a skill:** open the **Skills** tab, pick a skill, fill in its parameters, and press **Execute**. Arm skills are also in the **Manual Control** dropdown.
- **Switch agent:** on the **Home** screen, tap an agent card. MARS ships with a few, such as **Demo Agent** (chats and follows your gaze) and **Security Guard** (patrols and warns you about people).
