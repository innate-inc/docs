## Ideas to build

- A desk companion that nudges you when you pick up your phone.
- A companion that reminds your grandparents to take their medicine.
- A robot that picks up toys and drops them in a bin.
- A security robot that patrols and alerts you when it sees someone.
- A concierge that greets visitors and guides them around.
