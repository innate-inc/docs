**Talk to a robot, teach it new skills in Python, and watch it decide when to use them.** The simulator runs Innate OS, the full software of a real robot, in a 3D world on your laptop.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Try it in your browser" icon="globe" href="https://sim.innate.bot">
    Nothing to install. Join the queue and take MARS for a spin.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Run it on your laptop" icon="terminal" href="/simulator/setup">
    One command, about 10 minutes. You need this to build your own skills and agents.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

```bash
curl -fsSL https://link.innate.bot/sim | sh
```

Works on macOS, Linux, and Windows with WSL2. To chat with the agent, get a free [Gemini API key](https://aistudio.google.com/api-keys) before you start.

## For roboticists

Under the web app is a full ROS 2 stack, with the same topics, message types, and frames as the real hardware. Connect [Foxglove](/simulator/working-with-the-simulator#foxglove-and-ros-tooling) and your own ROS tools, or skip ROS and Docker entirely: [`VirtualMars`](/simulator/working-with-the-simulator#the-simulated-world-as-a-python-object) gives you the whole world as a Python object for scripts and reinforcement learning.

![Foxglove showing the simulated robot's cameras, map, LiDAR, and frames](/images/simulator/foxglove.png)

Physics runs in [MuJoCo](https://mujoco.org). Everything you build here also runs, unchanged, on a real [MARS](/robots/mars).
