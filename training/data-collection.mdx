You teleoperate the robot through the task while it records both cameras and the arm's joints at 30 Hz. Each recording is an episode. You need a [leader arm](/robots/mars/control-and-connectivity#leader-arm).

Train on your dataset, then [share it on Hugging Face](#share-on-hugging-face) so others can see what MARS can do, reuse your episodes, and train their own policies.

![The web app's Collect page](/images/main/robots/web-app/collect.jpg)

## Record episodes

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web app">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create the skill">
        On the **Collect** page, open the skill picker, choose **+ New skill… → Learned skill**, and name it.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Engage the leader arm">
        Plug the leader arm into your computer and click **Connect arm**, then **Engage follow**. Use Chrome or Edge.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Record">
        Press **Record**, do the task, press **Stop**, then **Save** or **Discard**. Repeat.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Phone app">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create the skill">
        Go to **Skills → Physical**, tap **+**, enter a name, and tap **Create Physical Skill**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Turn on the arm">
        Tap **Record Episode** and toggle **Arm** on. **Record** unlocks once arm data flows.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Record">
        Press **Record**, do the task, press **Stop**, then **Save** or **Discard**. Repeat.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The head stays locked while recording, so every episode has the same view.

## Record good data

The policy copies exactly what you do, mistakes included.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Start every episode from about the same arm pose | Start from wherever the arm happens to be |
| Move smoothly and deliberately | Hesitate or correct mid-motion. Discard those episodes. |
| Shift the object 2–5 cm between episodes | Put it in exactly the same spot every time |
| Keep the wrist camera pointed at the target | Let the object leave the camera's view |
| Keep lighting and background the same | Record some episodes with a sunny window and some without |

**How many?** At least 30 clean episodes for a simple task, 50–80 to be comfortable, and 150 or more for varied positions, objects, or lighting. 30 clean episodes beat 80 sloppy ones.

If the task involves driving, turn on base movement in the recorder. The episode then also records velocity commands and odometry.

## Review and upload for training

Replay your episodes and delete bad ones: on the web app's **Datasets** page, or the skill's **Record** tab in the phone app. New episodes show **preparing** while they encode.

To upload for [cloud training](/training/train-act-policy), tap **Upload** in the phone app's **Record** tab and wait for the green sync badge. The web app uploads automatically when you start training. Only new episodes are sent.

## Share on Hugging Face

<img src="/images/main/training/hugging-face-logo.svg" alt="Hugging Face" className="block dark:hidden" style={{ width: "220px" }} />
<img src="/images/main/training/hugging-face-logo-dark.svg" alt="Hugging Face" className="hidden dark:block" style={{ width: "220px" }} />

Publish your dataset to the [Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/datasets) as a standard LeRobot dataset. Anyone can then browse your episodes in LeRobot's [dataset visualizer](https://huggingface.co/spaces/lerobot/visualize_dataset), and train on them with [LeRobot](/training/lerobot). For an example, see [innate-inc/mars-pick-tv](https://huggingface.co/datasets/innate-inc/mars-pick-tv).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your token">
    Create a token with **write** permission at [huggingface.co/settings/tokens](https://huggingface.co/settings/tokens). In the web app, save it under **Settings → Keys → Hugging Face**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    On the **Datasets** page, open your dataset and press **Publish to Hugging Face**. The first time, the robot downloads the LeRobot environment, about 1.5 GB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Pick your account or an organization, and a repository name. **Private** is ticked by default: untick it to share the dataset with everyone. Only public datasets open in the visualizer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Publishing runs in the background, so you can close the dialog and reopen it to check progress. After you record more episodes, publish again: only the new ones are uploaded. Deleted episodes, and ones marked as failed, are left out of the next publish. Datasets you recorded before Innate OS 0.8.0 publish too.
