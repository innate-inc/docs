MARS is a compact mobile manipulator: a differential-drive base, a 5-joint arm with a gripper, a tilting head, and a Jetson computer that runs everything onboard. The cloud only handles the agent's model call and [policy training](/training/overview).

![MARS taken apart: LiDAR, depth camera with tilt, USB extension ports, Jetson Orin Nano, body with speaker, wheels and power distribution, the 5+1 DoF arm with its wide camera, and the battery](/images/main/robots/mars-overview.png)

## Specs

| Part | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Compute | [Jetson Orin Nano Super 8GB](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/autonomous-machines/embedded-systems/jetson-orin/nano-super-developer-kit/), 67 TOPS, 6-core ARM CPU, 1 TB SSD |
| Head camera | Stereo RGB, 150° diagonal FOV, depth from 40 cm to 6 m (under 2% error to 3.5 m) |
| Wrist camera | RGB, 160° FOV, 1920×1080 at 30 FPS |
| LiDAR | 2D, 360°, 0.15–6 m range, 6 Hz (up to 10 Hz) |
| Arm | 40 cm reach, 2 mm repeatability, 250 g payload at full extension |
| Expansion | 2 powered USB 3.0 ports (1.2 A each), plus GPIO and 5V/12V terminals. See [Extending MARS](/robots/mars/extending-mars). |

<Accordion title="Motors">
  Seven Robotis Dynamixel servos, all with 4096 steps per revolution. Motors 1–6 are the arm (6 is the gripper) and motor 7 is the head.

  | Motors | Model |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1, 3 | [`XL430-W250-T`](https://www.robotis.us/dynamixel-xl430-w250-t/) |
  | 2 | [`XC430-T240BB-T`](https://www.robotis.us/dynamixel-xc430-t240bb-t/) |
  | 4–7 | [`XL330-M288-T`](https://www.robotis.us/dynamixel-xl330-m288-t/) |
</Accordion>

## What it can do

| Capability | How | Learn more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navigate with a map | LiDAR localization on a saved map. Needed for spatial memory. | [Driving](/robots/mars/control-and-connectivity#navigation-modes) |
| Navigate without a map | Obstacle avoidance from onboard sensors only. | [Driving](/robots/mars/control-and-connectivity#navigation-modes) |
| Manipulate | Trained policies, or code using the arm API. | [Training](/training/overview), [Movement API](/software/skills/code-defined-skills/body-control-interfaces) |
| Talk and listen | Speaker, microphone, and the Innate agent. | [How the agent decides](/software/agent-loop) |
| Remember places | Stores views tied to the map and searches them. | [Spatial memory](/software/skills/code-defined-skills/spatial-memory) |
