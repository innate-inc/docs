---
title: "Stereo Depth Calibration"
description: "Calibrate the head camera's depth so navigation can avoid obstacles"
---

Each robot's stereo cameras differ slightly, so depth needs calibrating once per robot. Redo it after an update with depth changes, or when depth looks wrong. Without a calibration, navigation falls back to non-depth mode.

<iframe width="100%" height="420" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/V-20kHm7PHw" title="Self-calibrating depth camera" frameBorder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" allowFullScreen />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the board">
    Print the [calibration board](https://github.com/innate-inc/web-docs/blob/main/.gitbook/assets/mars-calibration-board.pdf) at **100% scale** and glue it flat onto letter-sized cardboard. Don't cover any squares or markers.

    <Frame><img src="/images/main/robots/mars/mars-stereo-calibration-board.png" width="300" /></Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start calibration">
    In the app, go to **Configuration → Depth** and tap **Start calibration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand MARS the board">
    When MARS raises its hand, hold the board in front of it with the pattern facing the head. Keep it steady until MARS grabs it, then step back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Wait for the app to report completion, then tap **Check depth**. If calibration fails, try again with a flatter board.
  </Step>
</Steps>
