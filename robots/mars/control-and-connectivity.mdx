You can drive MARS from the [phone app](/robots/apps#phone-app) (**Manual Control**) or the [web app](/robots/apps#web-app) (**Teleop**, joystick or `WASD`). Driving is ramped: releasing the stick or losing Wi-Fi slows the robot down smoothly instead of cutting out.

## Speed modes

| Mode | Multiplier | Top speed (default) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slow** | 0.35× | 0.14 m/s |
| **Med** | 0.65× | 0.26 m/s |
| **Fast** | 1.0× | 0.4 m/s |
| **Mad** | 2.0× | 0.8 m/s |

The multiplier scales `motion_control.max_speed` from [settings](/software/configuration). The mode is stored on the robot, so every app sees the same one. Recording an episode or a map holds the robot at **Med**.

<Warning>
  **Mad** goes past the configured limit. Use it only in open space.
</Warning>

## Leader arm

The leader arm is the small controller arm that ships with MARS. Move it and MARS's arm copies the joint angles. Squeeze the trigger at the tip to open and close the gripper.

It has no camera and its proportions differ from the robot's arm. Neither matters: training records the robot's joints and cameras, not the leader arm's.

## Navigation modes

| Mode | What it does | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Navigation** | Localizes on a saved map with LiDAR | You want repeatable paths and spatial memory |
| **Mapfree** | Avoids obstacles with onboard sensors, no map | You are in a new or changing space |
| **Mapping** | Builds a new map as you drive | You moved to a new space |

MARS starts mapping by itself the first time it connects. To make a new map, go to **Configuration → Mapping → Create New Map**, drive slowly through every room and doorway, then save. Keep the arm folded while mapping.

For developers, the current mode is published on `/nav/current_mode`.
