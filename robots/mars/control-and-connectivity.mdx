You can drive MARS from the [phone app](/robots/apps#phone-app) (**Manual Control**) or the [web app](/robots/apps#web-app) (**Teleop**, joystick or `WASD`). Driving is ramped: releasing the stick or losing Wi-Fi slows the robot down smoothly instead of cutting out.

## Speed modes

| Mode | Multiplier | Top speed (default) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slow** | 0.35× | 0.14 m/s |
| **Med** | 0.65× | 0.26 m/s |
| **Fast** | 1.0× | 0.4 m/s |
| **Mad** | 2.0× | 0.8 m/s |

The multiplier scales `motion_control.max_speed` from [settings](/software/configuration). The mode is stored on the robot, so every app sees the same one. Recording an episode or a map holds the robot at **Med**.

<Warning>
  **Mad** goes past the configured limit. Use it only in open space.
</Warning>

## Leader arm

The leader arm is the small controller arm that ships with MARS. Move it and MARS's arm copies the joint angles. Squeeze the trigger at the tip to open and close the gripper.

It has no camera and its proportions differ from the robot's arm. Neither matters: training records the robot's joints and cameras, not the leader arm's.

Stand behind the robot while teleoperating, so left and right match.

When nothing is using the arm, it folds itself to rest about 5 seconds after boot and whenever its torque comes back on. Keep the space around it clear. To turn this off, set `auto_rest: false` under `mars_arm` in [`config/settings.yaml`](/software/configuration).

## Phone teleop

No leader arm at hand? Move the arm by moving your phone instead. In the phone app's **Manual Control**, hold the fingerprint pad on the **Hold to move the arm** bar, then move your phone: the gripper follows your hand. Slide your thumb right along the bar to close the gripper, and left to open it.

<Frame><img src="/images/main/robots/mars/control-via-app-phone-teleop.jpg" width="300" alt="The phone app's Manual Control screen: camera views, the Hold to move the arm bar, the head slider, and the joystick" /></Frame>

The arm follows your hand from where the phone was when you pressed. Let go and the arm stays put. The next hold starts from there, so you can move your hand back and carry on.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Phones** | iPhone (ARKit), and Android phones that support ARCore. On Android the app offers to install Google Play Services for AR if it's missing. |
| **Needs** | Controller app 1.5.0 or newer, and Innate OS 0.8.0 or newer |
| **First hold** | If the arm is folded, it first moves to its zero pose (about 3 seconds, **Zeroing the arm…**), then follows. |
| **Tracking** | The phone tracks itself with its back camera. Aim it at a room with things in it, not a blank wall. If the bar says **Phone tracking not working**, tap it for help. |

## Navigation modes

| Mode | What it does | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Navigation** | Localizes on a saved map with LiDAR | You want repeatable paths and spatial memory |
| **Mapfree** | Avoids obstacles with onboard sensors, no map | You are in a new or changing space |
| **Mapping** | Builds a new map as you drive | You moved to a new space |

MARS starts mapping by itself the first time it connects. To make a new map, go to **Configuration → Mapping → Create New Map**, drive slowly through every room and doorway, then save. Keep the arm folded while mapping.

For developers, the current mode is published on `/nav/current_mode`.

### Localization

In **Navigation** mode, MARS finds where it is on the map from one LiDAR scan when it starts, then keeps track of it as it drives. If the position on the map looks wrong, relocate it:

| App | Automatic | By hand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web app map | **Relocate → Auto** | **Relocate → Manual**, then drag from where the robot is toward where it faces |
| Phone app map | **Locate → Auto-locate** | **Locate → Set Pose**, then drag the same way |

MARS also checks its position against what the LiDAR sees. If the two stop matching, for example because you picked it up and carried it, the robot reports **lost** and searches the map again as soon as it stands still. The web app's localization badge shows its verdict: **localized**, **low confidence**, or **lost**.

### Keepout zones

Keepout zones are areas MARS never drives into on its own, like a rug it gets stuck on. On the web app map, click **Keep Out** and paint the area. **Erase** removes part of a zone and **Clear** removes them all. Zones are saved with the map and don't change it.
