The [hardware is open source](https://github.com/innate-inc/mars), and the robot has room for your own parts.

| Port | Details |
| --- | --- |
| USB-A | 4 ports: 2 on the Jetson and 2 powered on the top hub |
| Jetson GPIO | Most pins are free for I2C, SPI, and UART. UART can cause audio artifacts. |
| Microcontroller GPIO | Unused pins are broken out on the PCB. See the [firmware repo](https://github.com/innate-inc/mars-firmware). |
| Power | Screw terminals for 12 V and 5 V |

## Add a sensor

Mount the device, plug it in, then feed its data to the agent with an [input device](/software/inputs). These work out of the box:

- [TONOR directional microphone](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CSCT63BL), for better voice pickup than the built-in mic
- [Blackiot Polverine](https://blackiot.swiss/polverine) air quality sensor

## Change the end effector

Nothing in the OS changes as long as you keep the last motor. Trained policies do need new data, because the gripper looks and moves differently.
